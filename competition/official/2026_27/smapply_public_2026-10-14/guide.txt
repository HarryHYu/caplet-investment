Investment Competition Guide
Where Do We Begin?
Start by reviewing the Investment Competition Guide with your entire team.
The guide provides an overview of the 10-week competition, explains how the Trading Notes Analysis, Investment Policy Statement, and Final Report work together, and identifies the key dates and expectations you will need to follow.
Reading the guide at the beginning will help your team understand the full competition journey before you begin researching investments or making portfolio decisions. Keep it nearby and return to it throughout the competition to make sure your work remains focused, connected, and on schedule.
