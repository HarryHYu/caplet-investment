Client Case Study
Get to the know the client. The Client Case Study is the starting point for the competition.
It introduces the client, her background, financial circumstances, and long-term goals. Teams should use this information to understand who they are investing for and to develop a thoughtful investment strategy tailored to her needs. The case study provides the foundation for the team’s portfolio decisions, Investment Policy Statement, Trading Notes Analysis, and Final Report.
