Wharton Global High School Investment Competition
An investment in knowledge pays the best interest.
Welcome to the 2026-2027 Wharton Global High School Investment Competition!
The Wharton Global High School Investment Competition is a free, experiential investment challenge for high school students (9th to 12th grade) and teachers. Students work in teams of four to six, guided by a teacher from their school as their advisor, and have access to an online stock market simulator. Together, they learn about strategy-building, teamwork, communication, risk, diversification, company and industry analysis, and many other aspects of investing.
